Questions people ask

Why do you show prices online?

Because you should know about what a new AC costs before anyone comes to your home. A big purchase is easier when you can see the numbers first, with no pressure.

Is this my final price?

It's an estimate. We confirm your final price at a free visit to your home, before any work starts. If something at your home changes the price, we explain why and give you the new price in writing first.

Which brand will I get?

Your quote lists the exact brand and model before you decide. The big brands are built much the same, so we focus on the right type of system for your home and a clean, careful install.
