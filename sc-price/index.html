What changes the price of a new AC

Two homes on the same street can pay very different prices. Here's what moves the number.

1. The size of your home

A bigger home needs a bigger system, measured in "tons." Too small and it never cools. Too big and it turns on and off and leaves the air sticky. We size it to your home at the visit, not by guessing.

2. The type of system

Most homes have one unit inside and one outside (a split system). Some have one outdoor unit that does it all (a package unit), and some condos have one indoor unit that uses the building's water to cool (water source). Then you pick how it runs: one speed, two speeds, or variable speed. More speeds cost more, run quieter, and pull more moisture out of the air.

3. Your ductwork

We check your ducts at the visit. If they need work, we show you why and give you the price in writing before anything is done.

What about the brand?

We install all brands, including Trane, Carrier, Goodman, Rheem and Lennox. The big brands are built much the same today, so we'd rather spend your money on the right size and a clean, careful install. Your quote lists the exact brand and model.

FPL rebate

We're on FPL's list of participating AC contractors. FPL offers a $200 rebate on qualifying full-system replacements (not mini-splits). It shows up as a credit on your invoice. Ask us if yours qualifies. FPL's list is not an endorsement of any contractor.

Questions people ask

Why do you show prices online?

Because you should know about what a new AC costs before anyone comes to your home. A big purchase is easier when you can see the numbers first, with no pressure.

Is this my final price?

It's an estimate. We confirm your final price when we come out for your free estimate, before any work starts. If something at your home changes the price, we explain why and give you the new price in writing first.

Which brand will I get?

We install all brands, including Trane, Carrier, Goodman, Rheem and Lennox. Tell us if you have a favorite. Your quote lists the exact brand and model before you decide. The big brands are built much the same, so we focus on the right type of system for your home and a clean, careful install.

What's included?
  • Taking out and hauling away your old system
  • Permit and inspection
  • New pad or stand, with hurricane straps where needed
  • Start-up test and full system check
  • Most installs are done in one day
How long does it take?

Most new system installs are done in one day. If yours needs more time, we tell you up front.

Is financing available?

Yes. Financing is available on qualifying installs, subject to lender approval.
